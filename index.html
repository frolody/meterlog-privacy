Privacy Policy

App Name: MeterLog: Electricity & Water
Effective Date: November 17, 2026

[YOUR_DEVELOPER_NAME] built the MeterLog app as an ad-supported, offline-first utility meter tracking application. This page informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our app.

1. Local Data Storage (No Account Required)

MeterLog is designed to work offline. All utility records you create—including electricity and water meter readings, prepaid token logs, custom tariff rates, notes, preferences, and scheduled reminders—are stored locally on your device. We do not operate external servers and do not collect, transmit, or store your personal meter data on our servers.

2. Device Permissions We Use

  • Photos and Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly when you choose to attach a photo of your electricity or water meter to a reading record. Selected photos remain stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications & Boot Receiver (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to display local notifications for utility meter reading reminders that you configure and to restore those local schedules after your device restarts.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to load and display advertisements via Google AdMob.

3. Backups & Data Exports

When you export your data to CSV or create a JSON backup, the file is saved directly to the folder you select on your device or storage provider via Android's Storage Access Framework. The developer has no access to your exported backup files.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

MeterLog uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information (such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, crash diagnostics, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google handles data in their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services

5. Children's Privacy

MeterLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
